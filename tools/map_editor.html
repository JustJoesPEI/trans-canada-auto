Trans-Canada Auto map editor

Fix streets, buildings and props from above. Your fixes are saved to data/edits.json, and the game lays them over the map data when it loads.

Press Open project folder and pick the trans-canada-auto folder (the one with project.godot in it). Use Chrome or Edge to save straight into the folder. In other browsers, Save downloads edits.json for you to put in the data folder.

How to use it

Moving around

Drag empty ground (or right-drag, or hold Space) to pan. Mouse wheel zooms. Type a street or building name in the search box, or a spot as x, z in game metres or lat, lon, the same numbers the game's N notes give.

Fixing a street

Select (V), click the street. Its points appear. Drag a point to move it. A point where streets meet moves for all of them. Drop a point onto another street's point to join them there (hold Alt to stop it snapping). Double-click the street's line to add a point. Click a point and press Delete to remove it. Change the name, type, lanes and one-way in the panel, or delete the street.

New street

Road (R): click along the way. Start or end on another street to join it. Enter or double-click finishes, Backspace takes back a point, Esc cancels.

Buildings

Select a building to change its height, roof, colours and material. Drag inside it to move it, drag a corner to reshape it, double-click an edge for a new corner. Building (B): click the corners and click the first one again (or Enter) to close it. Hold Shift to keep the corners square.

Props and trees

Props (P): pick one from the list and click to place it. Select a prop and turn it with [ and ], or the Facing box. Erase (E): click a lidar tree, or one of the hydrants, lamps and parked cars the game puts along the streets by itself, to take it out; click its red cross to put it back. To delete a prop you placed (or a mapped tree, stop sign, crosswalk), select it and press Delete. New trees are in the Props list.

Fences, walls and signs

Line (L): pick a fence, wall, hedge or guardrail and click along it. Signs are in the Props list: road signs, and business pylons, monument signs, wall signs and billboards with your own text and colours.

Where you start, and testing

Start (S): click and drag to set where the player starts and which way they face, or add the cars waiting at the start. Test here: click and drag anywhere, and the next time you run the game you start right there (in a car if you like), so you can check a fix in seconds. Clear it in the panel to start normally.

Seeing your changes in the game

Easiest: run the game from Godot (F5) and press F8 in the game. This editor opens already connected: no folder to pick, it saves straight into the project, Live is on, and a blue "You" marker shows where you are (Find me or Home centres on it).
Opened from the file instead: run the game from Godot (F5) and leave it running. Every time you save here, it rebuilds the town round you with your changes in a second or two, without restarting. Tick Live to save by itself after each change. Test here takes you straight to the spot you click (driving, if you tick "Start in a car").

Asking for a fix

Note (N): drag a loop round the spot and write what's wrong or what should be there. Notes are saved with your edits (the game ignores them) and list the streets and buildings inside, so Claude can find the exact spot. Select a note and press Copy for Claude to paste it into a chat, or just say "look at my map notes" after committing data/edits.json.

Saving

Ctrl+S saves. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo. The Changes tab lists every fix; click one to go there, or undo just that one. The game picks up the edits next time it starts. Commit data/edits.json to keep them.
